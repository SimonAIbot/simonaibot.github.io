Privacy Policy

AI Assistant is a private tool used only by its owner, on their own Google account.

  • Google user data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets) is accessed only to perform tasks the owner requests.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AI Assistant's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
